Status: PASS

Base: `116e5c3` (Runtime Bridge stage)

## Admission rule

Only `candidate_assessment` and `independent_success` Tutor events produce an
Elite Evidence record. They reuse `elite-evidence.schema.json` and ruleset
`elite-v1.1`; no parallel evidence model was introduced. Ordinary exposure,
task, learner submission, hint, feedback, mistake, checkpoint, idle and finalize
events remain lifecycle observations.

Tutor evidence is always appended with `confirmed: false`. Even when the tutor
reports an independent correct response, it cannot independently raise mastery
or readiness. The external Tutor event and its derived candidate evidence share
one journal transaction and one raw-input hash: both commit or both roll back.

## Mapping

- Tutor event/session/task/attempt/capability IDs remain explicit provenance.
- Correctness maps conservatively to the existing PASS/PARTIAL/FAIL/
  NOT_RECORDED values.
- Actual assistance, independence and agent usage are retained.
- Failure tags must use the existing registered failure taxonomy.
- Tutor evaluator confidence and reasoning quality remain metadata, not mastery.
- Exact retry is idempotent for both the Tutor event and candidate evidence.

## Non-regression

The bridge growth from 139 to 158 lines is limited to same-transaction candidate
admission. The mapping is isolated in `gallop/tutor/evidence.py` with its own
reviewed growth boundary. V1 replay remains exact; the v1 queue is not refreshed
or mutated by Tutor candidate admission; confirmed evidence semantics are
unchanged.
