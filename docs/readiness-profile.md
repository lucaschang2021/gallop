# Readiness Profile

The derived contract is
[`readiness-profile.schema.json`](../gallop/schemas/readiness-profile.schema.json),
with subject dimensions in
[`readiness-dimensions.json`](../gallop/schemas/readiness-dimensions.json).
Status and confidence are separate. Every non-UNKNOWN row has explicit evidence
refs and count. Thresholds and limits are in the
[Elite Training Protocol](elite-training-protocol.md).
