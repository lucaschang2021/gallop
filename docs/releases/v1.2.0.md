# Gallop v1.2.0

Release date: 2026-09-16  
Tag: `v1.2.0`  
Verified commit: `5c5ded1ecf163707fe818cbfea277abaa7b998b5`

Gallop v1.2.0 is the stable Zero-Touch Learning Continuity release. It keeps
Gallop headless and local-first while connecting four subject-bound GPT Tutor
surfaces to one authoritative Journal.

## Highlights

- Mathematics, Statistics & Econometrics, Finance, and CS & AI Tutor MCP
  surfaces with process-level subject isolation.
- Journal-derived bounded context restore for fresh chats and process restarts.
- Incremental checkpoints, abrupt-close recovery, and exact duplicate-event
  idempotency.
- Privacy-filtered, owned Obsidian projections and one-way Gallop-Reader
  continuity with fail-closed identity and ownership checks.
- Conservative evidence authority: Tutor assessments remain candidate evidence;
  synthetic fixtures never become learner mastery evidence.

## Boundaries

- DeepTutor is optional legacy compatibility and has zero required v1.2 runtime
  dependencies.
- Gallop ships no learner UI, no mandatory secondary AI, and no background
  scheduler.
- Competition Mathematics and Yau specialization are outside this release.
- The Mathematics independent retest remains `PARTIAL`, `GUIDED`, mastery `0`;
  the release preserves that result rather than lowering the evidence standard.
